Sound familiar?

You hired people to share the load.
Somehow it still lands with you.

  • “Even small decisions wait for me.”
  • “I delegate something, and it ends up back on my desk.”
  • “I answer the same questions again and again.”
  • “Things get dropped in handovers, and I’m the one who picks them up.”
  • “If I’m away for a few days, work slows down.”

How I help

Hands-on and with your team, not just with you: we start from how the work actually gets done.

  • Clarify responsibilities

    We look at who owns what today, where work waits for you and where it bounces back, then agree clear responsibilities.

  • Agree who decides what

    Together with the team, we agree which decisions people can make on their own, which need you, and who has the final say when it’s unclear.

  • Put usable processes in place

    We write down the few processes that matter most, such as onboarding, handovers or decision rules, in the tools you already use, and walk the team through them.

Making space for your team to thrive, and for you to lead.
