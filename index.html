01What can we work on together?

I help with positioning and digital presence, operations and process clarity, people and team systems, responsible AI, and the practical infrastructure that helps a small business or growing team work well.

02How is the free Team Snapshot different from the Clarity Check?

The free Team Snapshot is a two-minute self-assessment: six questions, an instant score for each area, practical tips and a PDF to keep. The paid Clarity Check goes deeper into one defined challenge: we talk it through, I review the documents and processes involved, and you get a written priority plan and a follow-up conversation.

03Can we begin with something small?

Yes. We can start with one question or a Clarity Check. If the Clarity Check shows problems that keep coming back, a Focused Build can tackle up to three of them next. Any larger piece of work is decided after the first step, rather than committed to up front.

04Do you work remotely or in person?

Most strategy and build work can happen remotely. I’m also available in person when that would be useful for your team. Workshops can be remote or in person by agreement.

05What happens after the first conversation?

If there is a useful fit, I will recommend a format and make the scope, timing, responsibilities, and fee clear before any work begins. If another direction would serve you better, I will say that too.

06How do team workshops work?

Workshops are shaped around one topic and combine practical exercises, discussion and next steps.
