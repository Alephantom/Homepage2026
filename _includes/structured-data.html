{%- comment -%} Schema.org structured data (JSON-LD): one @graph per page. Included from seo.html, which sets canonical_url, document_title, seo_description, seo_image(_width/_height) and page_language. Only facts that are visible on the site belong here: no address, phone number, social profiles, credentials, ratings or reviews. Noindex pages (404, newsletter confirmation) get no structured data. Every indexable page: a WebPage (ContactPage / CollectionPage via schema_page_type in the front matter) and, except on the homepages, a BreadcrumbList (Home → [breadcrumb_parent] → page; posts: Home → Journal → post). Names come from breadcrumb_title or title. Homepages (translation_key: home): WebSite and the full Person (@id /#person). Posts, /blog/ and pages with services repeat a short Person (name, url), so the author or provider can be read on its own; other pages only refer to /#person. Services: the keys in the page's schema_services, from _data/schema_services.yml, with prices read from _data/offer_prices.yml. A service's url and @id come from its path, so they are the same on every page that lists it. FAQPage (schema_faq: true): the same data file as the visible FAQ. Posts: BlogPosting, part of the Blog "Human in the Loop" (@id /blog/#blog). The cover image is the post's image, not primaryImageOfPage, because the page doesn't show it; posts without a cover have no image. isBasedOn: the original publication (original_url), which the post
