02 · After your Clarity Check

Focused Build
Fix what keeps coming back.

{{ site.data.offer_prices.focused_build.en | escape }}

2 weeks · 2 half-day sessions

Who it’s for

Teams who’ve had a Clarity Check and want the problems that keep coming back fixed: dropped handovers, onboarding that hangs on one person, every question landing with the founder.

What’s included

  • A deeper analysis of your people and set-up: roles, responsibilities, processes and tools
  • The pain points behind your priorities, named and ranked; together we pick up to three to fix
  • Two half-day working sessions with the people involved, to shape the new processes with them
  • Written processes and guidelines for each issue, such as an onboarding checklist, a handover template or decision rules, in the tools you already use
  • A 2–4 hour handover with the team(s) involved, so everyone knows how the new ways of working run
Plan your Focused Build↗

Builds on your Clarity Check: we start from its priority plan.

Where it stops

Written processes and guidelines for up to three priority issues, plus a team handover. Anything else we find goes on a prioritised list for later; wider rollout and ongoing support are scoped separately.

What changes for you

Up to three recurring problems get a clear, documented way of working that your team understands and can run without me. You get back the time now spent patching the same things again and again.

Interim HR Business Partner
For the time you need.

On request

Time commitment, duration and fee agreed around your situation

Who it’s for

Founders and teams covering parental leave or sick leave, an open role or a period of change, who need someone to stay close to the work.

What’s included

  • Parental leave or sick leave cover and support between permanent hires
  • Organisational structure, responsibilities and People & Culture decisions
  • Employee lifecycle, team development and everyday people operations
  • A considered handover when your colleague returns or a new person joins
Let’s talk about interim support↗
Where it stops

We agree responsibilities, time commitment and duration up front. It ends with a considered handover when your colleague returns or a new person joins.

What changes for you

People processes keep moving and everyday decisions get made, so the gap doesn’t land on the founder. I work alongside you and bring structure to what comes next.
