3. Contact by email

If you contact me by email, I process the information you send—usually your name, email address, message, and any project details—to answer you and handle the enquiry. If your message concerns a possible or existing contract, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Otherwise, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; my legitimate interest is communicating with people who contact me.

I retain correspondence only for as long as it is needed for the enquiry and any resulting relationship, unless statutory retention duties require a longer period.

4. Contact form

The contact page offers a form for enquiries. When you send it, the details you enter—the topic you choose, your name, email address, company (optional) and message—are transmitted to Formspree, which forwards them to me by email and keeps a copy of the submission in my Formspree account. 6. Quarterly newsletter

{% if newsletter_url != "" %}

With your Team Snapshot results, you can sign up for my quarterly newsletter with practical ideas on people and process topics, plus occasional special offers. Signing up is optional and does not change your results.

When you sign up, your email address, the team or company name (if you entered one), your Snapshot scores—your overall score and the result for each area—and your consent are sent to Brevo. I use your email address to send you the newsletter and occasional special offers. Your company name and scores are stored with your subscription so that I know the context you signed up in and can keep what I send relevant.

The legal basis is your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR, which you give by ticking the consent box. After you sign up, you receive an email asking you to confirm your subscription (double opt-in). You are only added to the newsletter list once you confirm. To be able to prove your consent, Brevo records the time of your sign-up and of your confirmation, together with the IP address used each time. The legal basis for this record is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; my legitimate interest is being able to demonstrate your consent, as Article 7(1) GDPR requires.

The newsletter is managed and sent with Brevo, a service of Sendinblue SAS, Paris, France, in the European Union. Brevo stores and processes the data on my behalf as a processor. More information is available in Brevo’s Privacy Policy ↗.

You can withdraw your consent and unsubscribe at any time, using the link at the end of every newsletter or by emailing {{ site.legal.email }}. The withdrawal does not affect processing that occurred before it. I keep your data for the newsletter until you unsubscribe. After that, your email address stays on a block list so that you aren’t sent the newsletter again by mistake, and the record of your consent is kept for as long as I may need to prove it (normally up to three years). The legal basis for both is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Your email address and consent are needed to send you the newsletter. A sign-up from the Team Snapshot page always includes your Snapshot scores; the company name is optional.

7. Appointment booking with Calendly

The contact page offers an optional scheduling calendar from Calendly, LLC, 115 E Main St., Suite A1B, Buford, GA 30518, USA. The calendar is not loaded automatically. Only after you select “Load the scheduling calendar” does your browser connect to Calendly. Calendly may then process technical connection data and use cookies or similar storage. If you book a meeting, Calendly also processes the details you enter, such as your name, contact details, selected time, time zone, and any answers you provide.

The calendar is loaded on the basis of your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG. Booking data required to arrange the meeting is also processed under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Processing may take place in the United States. More information is available in Calendly’s Privacy Notice ↗. You can avoid the embedded calendar and contact me by email instead.
