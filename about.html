About · What I do

I turn complexity into
something people can work with.

I've worked across people, product, and delivery, which means I see the whole system: what employees need, what users experience, and what stakeholders can actually support.

Together, we turn that view into a sharper direction, better ways of working, and practical momentum—from the first useful question to the thing your team can carry forward.

A little about me

I care about the conditions that let good work happen.

I'm brought in when growth, change, or new technology has made the old way of working stop making sense. I help founders and teams find the real constraint, make confident decisions, and turn them into practical systems and ways of working people can actually use.
