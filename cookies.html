What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage, can remember information without using a cookie. Some storage is needed for a service you request; analytics storage is optional and requires your consent.

Managing browser data

You can also delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings. Blocking all browser storage may prevent the website from remembering your privacy choice. Loading the Calendly calendar is a separate action and does not change your analytics preference.
