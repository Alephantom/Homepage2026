A practical way forward

Strategy is most useful when it survives contact with the daily business and the people doing the work.

I work across people, operations, positioning, and delivery. That makes this support useful when the challenge does not fit neatly inside one discipline—and when the decision and its implementation need to stay connected.

A good fit when

You can see the symptoms, but the real constraint is unclear. Priorities compete, conversations loop, or the team keeps solving different versions of the same problem.

What we do

We start with a focused video conversation about what’s happening. Then I review your actual ways of working, including the documents, processes and responsibilities involved, to find where work gets stuck and why.

You leave with

A written priority plan showing what to address first, what can wait and why, plus a follow-up conversation to talk it through together. From there, you can continue with a Focused Build.

02

Fix what keeps coming back

Focused Build

{{ site.data.offer_prices.focused_build.en | escape }} · 2 weeks · 2 half-day sessions

The next step after a Clarity Check: two focused weeks for teams where the same people and process problems keep coming back.

Plan your Focused Build ↗

Builds on your Clarity Check: we start from its priority plan.

A good fit when

Your Clarity Check has shown where work gets stuck: handovers that drop things, onboarding that depends on one person, questions that always land with the founder. Now you want it fixed properly rather than patched again.

What we do

Building on your Clarity Check, I analyse your people and set-up in more depth (roles, responsibilities, processes and tools) and together we pick up to three pain points to fix. In two half-day working sessions with the people involved, we shape the new processes; I then write them up as guidelines your team can use. To keep it to two weeks, I need early access to the relevant documents and tools.

You leave with

Written processes and guidelines for up to three recurring problems, built so your team can run and maintain them, a prioritised list of everything else we found, and a 2–4 hour handover with the teams involved. Not a deck that creates another project after the project.
