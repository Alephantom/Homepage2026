A practical way forward

Strategy is most useful when it survives contact with the daily business and the people doing the work.

I work across people, operations, positioning, and delivery. That makes this support useful when the challenge does not fit neatly inside one discipline—and when the decision and its implementation need to stay connected.

01

Understand the problem

Clarity review

For a question that still feels tangled, broad, or strangely resistant to the fixes you have already tried.

A good fit when

You can see the symptoms, but the real constraint is unclear. Priorities compete, conversations loop, or the team keeps solving different versions of the same problem.

What we do

We review the context, map what is happening, test the assumptions around it, and separate the important signal from the surrounding noise.

You leave with

A shared view of the problem, a focused priority list, and practical next steps that make the next decision easier.

02

Create the useful thing

Focused build

For one defined area that needs to move from a good intention to a concrete, usable result.

A good fit when

The direction is clear enough, but the page, process, workflow, guidance, or small system still needs to be shaped and made real.

What we do

We define the useful outcome, make the necessary decisions, build the core piece, and test it against the people who need to use it.

You leave with

A working output your team can use and maintain—not a deck that creates another project after the project.

03

Stay close to the change

Interim HR Business Partner

For founders and growing teams who need experienced People & Culture support close to the decisions and the day-to-day work.

A good fit when

Growth or change has made the old people practices stop working, but a permanent senior hire is not yet the right answer.

What we do

I work alongside founders and leaders on priorities such as organisational structure, employee lifecycle, team development, and the people systems behind them.

You leave with

Clearer ownership, stronger foundations, and practical progress that can be carried by the team after the interim support ends.
